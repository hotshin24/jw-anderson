FAQ

Order

  1. 1. How do I place an order?

    Browse our collection and select the item you wish to purchase. Choose your preferred size and add the item to your shopping bag. Proceed to checkout and enter your shipping and payment details. Once your order is confirmed, you will receive a confirmation email.

  2. 2. Can I cancel or modify my order?

    You may request to cancel or modify your order before it has been dispatched. Once the order has been shipped, we are unable to make any changes. If you need assistance, please contact our customer service team as soon as possible. We will do our best to accommodate your request.

  3. 3. How do I check my order status?

    You can check your order status by logging into your account and visiting the “My Orders” section. Alternatively, you can use the tracking link provided in your shipping confirmation email. This will allow you to monitor your delivery in real time. Please note that tracking information may take some time to update after dispatch.

  4. 4. Will I receive an order confirmation?

    Yes, a confirmation email will be sent to you shortly after your order has been placed. This email will include your order number and purchase details. If you do not receive it, please check your spam or junk folder. You may also contact customer support for further assistance.

Shipping

  1. 5. What shipping options are available?

    We offer both standard and express shipping options depending on your location. Available methods and delivery times will be displayed at checkout. Shipping costs are calculated based on your destination and selected service. Please review all options before completing your purchase.

  2. 6. How long does delivery take?

    Delivery times vary depending on your location and the shipping method selected. Express shipping typically arrives within 1–3 business days. Standard shipping may take approximately 4–7 business days. Please note that delays may occur during peak seasons or due to customs processing.

  3. 7. How are shipping fees calculated?

    Shipping fees are calculated automatically at checkout based on your delivery address and selected shipping method. The total cost will be displayed before you complete your purchase. Additional duties or taxes may apply depending on your country. These charges are the responsibility of the customer.

  4. 8. Can I track my shipment?

    Yes, once your order has been dispatched, you will receive a shipping confirmation email. This email will include a tracking link for your shipment. You can use this link to monitor the delivery status at any time. Please allow some time for tracking information to become available.

Returns & Exchanges

  1. 9. What is your return policy?

    You may request a return within 14 days of receiving your order. Items must be unused, in their original condition, and returned with all original packaging. Returns that do not meet these conditions may not be accepted. Please ensure all items are carefully packed before returning.

  2. 10. How do I request a return?

    To request a return, log in to your account and navigate to the “My Orders & Returns” section. Select the item you wish to return and follow the instructions provided. Once your request is approved, you will receive return instructions. Please follow these carefully to ensure a smooth process.

  3. 11. Are all items eligible for return?

    Not all items are eligible for return due to hygiene or customization reasons. Items such as earrings, underwear, and personalized products are non–returnable. This policy ensures product quality and safety for all customers. Please review product details before making a purchase.

  4. 12. Can I exchange an item?

    Exchanges can be requested through the returns process. Availability of replacement items may vary depending on stock. If your desired item is unavailable, you may choose to return it for a refund instead. Our team will guide you through the process if needed.

Refunds

  1. 13. When will I receive my refund?

    Once your return has been received and inspected, your refund will be processed. This process may take several business days to complete. The refunded amount will be credited to your original payment method. Please note that processing times may vary depending on your bank or provider.

  2. 14. Are shipping fees refundable?

    Shipping fees are generally non–refundable once the order has been delivered. In cases of defective or incorrect items, exceptions may apply. Please contact our customer service team for assistance in such cases. We will review your request and provide further guidance.

Payment

  1. 15. What payment methods do you accept?

    We accept a variety of payment methods, including major credit and debit cards. Additional payment options may be available depending on your region. All transactions are processed securely to ensure your information is protected. Please check the checkout page for available options.

  2. 16. Why was my payment declined?

    A payment may be declined for several reasons, including incorrect details or insufficient funds. It may also be due to security checks by your bank or payment provider. Please verify your information and try again. If the issue persists, we recommend contacting your bank for further assistance.

Account

  1. 17. Do I need an account to place an order?

    You can place an order as a guest without creating an account. However, registering an account allows you to track your orders more easily. You can also save your details for faster checkout in the future. Additionally, you will have access to your order history.

  2. 18. How do I reset my password?

    To reset your password, click the “Forgot your password?” link on the login page. Enter your registered email address and follow the instructions sent to you. This will allow you to create a new password securely. If you do not receive the email, please check your spam folder.

Customer Support

  1. 19. How can I contact customer service?

    Our customer service team is available to assist you with any inquiries. You can contact us via email or through our contact form. We aim to respond within standard business hours. Please provide as much detail as possible to help us assist you efficiently.

Product

  1. 20. Will out–of–stock items be restocked?

    Some items may be restocked depending on availability and demand. However, certain limited or seasonal items may not return once sold out. We recommend checking back regularly for updates. You may also sign up for notifications if available.
