DRESSES

JW Anderson’s dresses for women extend from the earliest women’s collections, articulated and elevated hrough new fabrications and manipulations each season. Signature designs, such as the shirt dress, the paisley dress, the tie front dress and drape front dress, are reworked in seasonal colours and materials. Crafted in luxury materials: double duchess silk, embroidered sequins, lace, cotton, jersey and wool. Tie–detailing, asymmetrical silhouettes and draping run throughout. Each season classic, timeless designs are punctuated with standout runway pieces.
