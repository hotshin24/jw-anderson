DEAN SAMESHIMA - ‘ANONYMOUS’

Crafted between London and Portugal, these pieces feature bold, raised embroidery and intarsia in the knitwear, inspired by renowned artist Dean Sameshima. Created in collaboration with renowned artist Dean Sameshima, inspired by Anonymous, his series shown at the 2024 Venice Biennale. This collaboration translates his work into limited–edition graphic pieces. Sameshima’s work deals with subculture and identity; he collects, accumulates and reworks imagery of queer culture, punk and underground movements, their symbols introduced to subversive and unexpected contexts. A dialogue between art and fashion.

Art × Fashion Dialogue

Created between London and Portugal, the collection exists at the intersection of artistic expression and fashion design. Each piece translates visual language into wearable form, establishing a clear dialogue between disciplines.

Dean Sameshima Collaboration

Developed in collaboration with Dean Sameshima, the collection draws from his Anonymous series presented at the 2024 Venice Biennale. His work is reinterpreted into limited–edition graphic garments through considered craft and materiality.

Subculture & Identity

Sameshima’s practice explores themes of identity through queer culture, punk, and underground movements. These references are woven into the collection, introducing subcultural symbols within unexpected and refined contexts.

Graphic Expression

Bold, raised embroidery and graphic intarsia bring a strong visual impact to knitwear and garments. The pieces function as canvases, carrying layered imagery with clarity and intention.

Subversive Context

Collected and reworked symbols are repositioned to challenge convention and expectation. Familiar cultural references are transformed through scale, placement, and craft–led execution.

Limited–Edition Craft

Produced as a focused series of limited–edition pieces, the collection emphasises rarity and intention. Each garment reflects a precise translation of artistic work into fashion through collaboration and care.
