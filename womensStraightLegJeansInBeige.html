JAPANESE DENIM

Crafted in Hiroshima, Japan, developed in collaboration with expert Japanese denim makers, all denim pieces reflect a commitment to longevity, refinement, and heritage craft. JW Anderson has worked closely with Japanese artisans to create denim garments and accessories elevated through distinctive detailing: anchor rivets, orange binding, selvedge seams, and a new leather patch edged using a technique borrowed from traditional bookbinding. The collection also introduces Boro, a hand–patched repair method dating back to 19th Century Japan. Originally used as a means of extending the life of garments, Boro transforms utility into artistry, layering memory, texture, and time. From raw to stone–washed, and reimagined through accessories like the signature Loafer Bag, JW Anderson’s Japanese denim pays homage to an everyday material: lived in, to live in. An ordinary fabric made extraordinary through care, craft and intention.

Heritage Craft

Crafted in Hiroshima, Japan, each denim piece is rooted in generations of Japanese textile expertise and heritage workmanship. Traditional techniques are carried forward through a contemporary lens, preserving craft while allowing it to evolve.

Longevity

Designed to be worn, repaired, and lived in, the denim places durability and endurance at its core. Every garment reflects a commitment to lasting quality and thoughtful construction over time.

Artisanal Detail

Signature elements such as anchor rivets, orange binding, selvedge seams, and bookbinding–inspired leather patches define the collection. These details elevate each piece through precision, character, and refined craftsmanship.

Boro Expression

Drawing from a 19th–century Japanese hand–repair tradition, Boro introduces visible mending as an expressive design language. Layers of stitching and fabric transform function into artistry, carrying memory, texture, and time.

Everyday Elevated

From raw to stone–washed finishes, denim is reimagined across garments and accessories, including the signature Loafer Bag. Everyday materials are transformed into considered pieces designed for modern life.

Intentional Making

Developed in close collaboration with Japanese artisans, each piece reflects a deliberate and respectful approach to making. JW Anderson denim is shaped by care, craft, and clear design intention.
